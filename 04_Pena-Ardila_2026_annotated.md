## Theme 1: WHAT — "Donor sport" 概念 + 可迁移技能

> "The 'donor sport' concept proposes that engaging in futsal can facilitate the acquisition of transferable skills for association football (soccer), consistent with ecological dynamics perspectives on constraint-led learning."
>
> — Abstract

> "Futsal's inherent task constraints, including a smaller court size, high opponent pressure, reduced time for action, and the use of a smaller, low-bounce ball … induce specific technical, tactical, and cognitive adaptations that positively transfer to the 11-a-side game."
>
> — Introduction

> "In the ASM, donor sports are expected to enrich athletes' perceptual–motor landscapes by (i) developing broad coordinative foundations via a concentric approach, (ii) exploiting transferability through multisport/donor-sport engagement, and (iii) creating challenging environments that promote adaptability and perception–action coupling."
>
> — Theoretical framework

> "we operationalized transfer as evidence that futsal exposure (training interventions or developmental history) is associated with measurable changes in soccer-relevant performance outcomes."
>
> — Theoretical framework

## Theme 2: EVIDENCE — Meta 分析实证

> "The meta-analysis revealed a very large overall effect size (SMD = 1.43; 95% CI: 0.16 to 3.03) favoring futsal-based interventions. Although substantial statistical heterogeneity was observed (I2 = 86.9%, p < .001), the direction of the effect was consistently positive across all individual studies."
>
> — Meta-Analysis Results §Transfer to Passing Accuracy

> "the investigations with the highest level of experimental control (Oppici et al.) reported effect sizes exceeding 2.0, suggesting an acute and significant transfer of technical proficiency."
>
> — Meta-Analysis Results

> "Field-based assessments: Studies utilizing simulated game scenarios (Benvenuti et al., 2010) demonstrated large effect sizes favoring futsal players in reactive agility and decision-making speed (SMD > 1.50), suggesting a transfer of cognitive speed in representative environments."
>
> — Meta-Analysis Results §Perceptual-Motor Skills

> "Laboratory or isolated tasks: Conversely, investigations employing video-based reaction tests or linear dribbling drills without opposition … indicated superior or comparable performance by soccer players, shifting the effect direction toward the control group."
>
> — Meta-Analysis Results

> "a retrospective study on Brazilian professional female players found a significant correlation between the accumulated hours of deliberate practice in Futsal during childhood (6–12 years) and better offensive tactical decision-making quality in adulthood (Machado et al., 2024)."
>
> — Synthesis of Findings

> "Training with the Futsal ball was sufficient to accelerate the acquisition of passing skills and transfer them positively to performance with a standard soccer ball (Caglayan et al., 2018; Oppici et al., 2018b)."
>
> — Discussion

> "The Futsal ball possesses a lower coefficient of restitution (less bounce) and is considered easier to control than the soccer ball. This simplifies the kicking action, which in turn frees up the player's attentional resources to focus on processing information related to the environment."
>
> — Discussion

> "successful dribbles in Futsal were significantly associated with specific spatiotemporal coordination tendencies, namely the smallest interpersonal distances (ranging from 0.20 to 0.35 m) and the highest relative dribbling velocities. Crucially, the same spatiotemporal parameters that determined success in Futsal did not affect the dribbling outcomes in soccer."
>
> — Synthesis of Findings

> "success in soccer dribbling was found to be statistically related to the defender's Reaction Time (RT)"
>
> — Synthesis §Dribbling

## Theme 3: HOW — 实践建议 + 局限

> "Coaches should endorse the systematic integration of the Futsal ball and Futsal-like constraints (e.g., small-sided games) in youth development programs to maximize the acquisition and positive transfer of passing accuracy."
>
> — Conclusion §Practical Recommendations

> "The evidence supports the Specialized Diversification pathway, emphasizing that a high volume of deliberate practice in Futsal, especially in childhood and early adolescence, promotes the development of high-quality offensive decision-making skills in adult soccer."
>
> — Conclusion §Practical Recommendations

> "Reactive agility tests conducted under ecological, field-based conditions (RVS-T) can be reliable measures to identify players with superior, transferred decision-making and cognitive speed."
>
> — Conclusion

> "The transfer of speed-related perceptual-motor skills is highly dependent on context and task specificity."
>
> — Discussion

> "The quantitative synthesis is based on a limited number of included studies (N = 7) and demonstrated high statistical heterogeneity (e.g., I2 values exceeding 86% in both key quantitative analyses)."
>
> — Limitations

> "the prevalence of cross-sectional or observational designs in the primary literature, hindering the establishment of direct causality."
>
> — Limitations

> "The Futsal advantage, therefore, seems to be rooted in improving the perception-action coupling … rather than enhancing general visual processing speed."
>
> — Discussion
