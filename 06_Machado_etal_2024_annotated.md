## Theme 1: WHAT — 决策技能（非技术技能）

> "This is the first study to analyse the contribution of deliberate practice, play, and futsal to develop decision-making skills in female soccer players."
>
> — Abstract

> "decision-making can be defined as the ability of the player to select an adequate action from a range of possible alternatives to solve a given situation in the game."
>
> — Introduction

> "The characteristics of futsal, such as fewer players on the court, smaller playing area duration, continuous transitions and its particular rules, promote a large number of actions that require the players to decide and solve problems throughout the game."
>
> — Introduction

> "there is empirical evidence that futsal practice promotes the transfer of tactical-technical skills to soccer."
>
> — Introduction

> "perceptual-cognitive and perceptual-motor skills, where both contribute to the development of expertise in the sport."
>
> — Introduction

## Theme 2: EVIDENCE — 决策技能实证

> "We found differences among high skill (HS) and low skill (LS) decision-making groups regarding their participation in developmental activities in every period assessed (childhood, early, and late adolescence)."
>
> — Abstract

> "the HS group accumulated more percentage time of their participation in soccer activities in practice compared to the LS group."
>
> — Abstract

> "the HS group gathered more hours of practice in futsal and total practice (i.e., considering practice time in soccer and futsal) in early adolescence (13–15 years) compared to the LS group."
>
> — Abstract

> "We concluded that the specialised diversification pathway is the one that best characterises the sports involvement of better decision-makers during childhood."
>
> — Abstract

> "players who possessed better decision-making skills than their colleagues from the same competitive level accumulated more hours in soccer activities (i.e., team practice, competition, and play activities) during their childhood."
>
> — Introduction

> "The sample comprised 77 Brazilian professional female soccer players. Decision-making skill was assessed based on a video-based test (TacticUP®)."
>
> — Abstract

## Theme 3: HOW — 发展路径建议

> "the specialised diversification pathway is the one that best characterises the sports involvement of better decision-makers during childhood."
>
> — Abstract

> "the early engagement hypothesis, is characterised by involvement in the primary sport (soccer) in both activities of deliberate practice and deliberate play since the early years of sports participation (6–12 years)."
>
> — Introduction

> "the acquisition of superior decision-making skills in invasion sports is associated with sport-specific experience (e.g., team practice, competition, and play activities) and the quality of the experiences."
>
> — Introduction

> "the interaction of both activities (deliberate play and practice) is essential for the development of decision-making in athletes."
>
> — Introduction

> "talent development could assess players' previous futsal participation to further understand its association with the acquisition of decision-making skills."
>
> — Introduction
