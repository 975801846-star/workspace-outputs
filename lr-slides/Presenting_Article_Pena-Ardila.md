## How Was It Done?

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Guideline** | PRISMA 2020 |
| **Databases** | Scopus · ScienceDirect · Web of Science |
| **Screening** | 780 → 683 (dedup) → 38 (full-text) → **7 studies** |
| **Criteria** | PICO(S) — population, intervention, comparison, outcome, study design |
| **Analysis** | Random-effects meta-analysis · Hedges' g · I² |

### Key Numbers at a Glance

| Stat | Value | What It Means |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **SMD** (effect size) | **1.43** | Very large effect — futsal ball >>> normal ball |
| **95% CI** (reliability) | -0.16 ~ 3.03 | Wide interval; direction is right but need more studies |
| **I²** (agreement) | **86.9%** | Very high — study methods differ greatly → field still young |

---

## Three Main Findings

### <span class="tag-green">✅ PASSING: Strong Transfer</span>
- SMD = 1.43 — very large effect
- Low-bounce ball → easier control → frees attention
- Passing accuracy ↑ in soccer context

### <span class="tag-amber">⚠️ SPEED & AGILITY: Context-Dependent</span>
- Field-based tests → futsal players **faster**
- Lab-based tests → futsal = or **slower**
- Futsal trains perception-action coupling, **not** raw processing speed

### <span class="tag-red">❌ DRIBBLING: Sport-Specific</span>
- Futsal: close distance + high velocity
- Soccer: defender's reaction time
- Different constraints → different solutions — **no automatic transfer**

---

## Additional Finding & Limitations

### <span class="tag-blue">🧠 COGNITIVE DEVELOPMENT</span>
- Early futsal deliberate practice (6–12y, 13–15y)
  → higher **offensive decision-making quality** in adulthood
- Machado et al. (2023, 2024) — Brazilian professional female players

### ⚠️ Limitations (authors admitted)

| Gap | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| Small sample | **7 studies** only |
| Study design | Mostly cross-sectional — **no longitudinal tracking** |
| Dose-response | **How much** futsal is enough? Unknown |
| Language | EN / ES / PT only |

---

## My View as a Coach

### ✅ What I Can Use
- **Low-bounce ball** in youth training
- **Small-sided constraints** → force quick decisions
- **Early futsal exposure** → talent development

### ❓ Unanswered Questions
- **WHEN** to introduce futsal? At what age?
- **WHEN** to transition to larger-sided games?
- Must low-bounce drills be **indoor**? Does **grass friction** affect transfer?
- **Weaknesses** of adult futsal players switching to 11-a-side?
  → Offside awareness? Long passing? Goalkeeper transition?
- **Negative transfer** — does futsal create bad habits for football?

> Almost no research on negative transfer — a key gap my LR addresses.
