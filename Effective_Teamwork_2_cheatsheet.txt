==== Effective Teamwork 2 - 课堂讲解小抄 ====

【一、8 个选项 - 简单英语解释（照着念）】

E - Extroverts    "They like being with people and talking."
I - Introverts    "They like time alone and think quietly."
S - Sensing       "They trust facts and what is practical."
N - Intuition     "They trust ideas and imagination."
T - Thinking      "They use logic and analyse things."
F - Feeling       "They care about people and harmony."
J - Judging       "They like plans and clear decisions."
P - Perceiving    "They like to stay open and adapt."

【二、8 个角色 - 简单英语介绍（擅长什么 / 怕什么 / 你符合么）】

1. Coach (教练)
   "Coaches are good at creating a warm team atmosphere and motivating people. But they can be easily influenced. Does this sound like you?"

2. Conductor (指挥家)
   "Conductors are good at planning, organising and setting clear roles. But they can seem too controlling. Does this sound like you?"

3. Crusader (旗手)
   "Crusaders are good at standing up for strong values and beliefs. But they can clash with people who disagree. Does this sound like you?"

4. Explorer (探索者)
   "Explorers are good at trying new ways and challenging the status quo. But they get bored easily and ignore details. Does this sound like you?"

5. Innovator (创新者)
   "Innovators are good at creating new ideas and a long-term vision. But they can seem unrealistic. Does this sound like you?"

6. Curator (整理者)
   "Curators are good at collecting information and seeing things clearly. But they can be too cautious about new ideas. Does this sound like you?"

7. Sculptor (实干家)
   "Sculptors are good at getting things done quickly and pushing the team into action. But they can get impatient with others. Does this sound like you?"

8. Scientist (科学家)
   "Scientists are good at analysing and explaining how things work. But they prefer to work alone. Does this sound like you?"


【三、可能被问的问题 - 简单应答】

1. 做这个有什么用?
   "The point is self-awareness. When you know your own style and understand others are different, teamwork is easier."

2. 准不准?
   "It is not a perfect scientific test, but it is useful for starting a conversation about how we differ."

3. 人会变吗?
   "Yes, your type can change over time. The reflection questions ask if you agree with the result."

4. 团队要都有吗?
   "A mix is better. Different roles balance each other."
