## Theme 1: WHAT — 哪些技能可被迁移

### 1.1 Peña-Ardila (2026) — 系统综述 · 地图

- 'Donor sport' concept rooted in ecological dynamics & constraint-led learning (p. 2)
- Futsal constraints: smaller court, high opponent pressure, reduced time, smaller low-bounce ball → technical/cognitive adaptations (p. 2)
- Early diversification (DMSP framework) preferred over early specialisation (p. 2)
- Brazilian examples: Pelé, Ronaldinho, Neymar attributed skills to futsal background (p. 2)
- Meta-analysis: passing accuracy SMD = 1.43 (very large ES; 95% CI: −0.16 to 3.03) favouring futsal-based constraints (p. 1)
- 'Current evidence supports futsal as a potent medium to accelerate acquisition of technical passing precision' (p. 1)
- Time-based perceptual-motor: non-significant, highly heterogeneous (p. 1)
- 'Field-based reactive tasks tended to favour futsal, whereas isolated laboratory tasks tended to favour soccer' (p. 1)
- Transfer of speed and agility 'appears to be highly dependent on context and task specificity' (p. 1)
- Futsal demands quick, effective responses in unpredictable situations → faster DM & reactive agility (p. 3)

**在 Theme 1 中的角色：** 地图 — 系统盘点"什么技能可迁移"，给出 Meta effect size。

---

### 1.2 Travassos, Araújo and Davids (2018) — 理论框架 · 互补性机制

- Athlete development emerges through continuous experience of varied activities under different task & environmental constraints (p. 66)
- Early diversification enriches adaptive capacities; early specialisation risks physical, psychological & social problems including dropout (p. 66-67)
- Futsal + football share 'complementarity' — not simple one-direction transfer (p. 67)
- 'Playing football on the street, on the beach, on different fields with different formats' = variable practice (p. 66)
- Varied environmental & task constraints 'promote enrichment of learning process through enhanced transfer' (p. 66)
- 'Individual's behaviour depends on their capabilities and how they interact with context to perceive possibilities for action' (p. 68)
- Players become 'perceptually attuned to environmental information that specifies affordances' (p. 67)
- 'Self-evident advances toward task goals' → players discover individual capabilities through diverse play (p. 66)

**在 Theme 1 中的角色：** 理论解释 — 解释"为什么"能迁移（相似的 perception-action coupling + 互补性机制）。

---

### 1.3 Yiannaki, Toering, Collins and Carling (2026) — 教练视角

- Futsal has become prominent research focus due to unique potential for enhancing football TD (p. 1)
- Ecological dynamics framework: behaviours shaped through player-environment interactions; perception & action coupled directly (p. 2)
- 'Similarities between sports are deemed an obligatory requirement for optimising transfer' (p. 1)
- Futsal constraints (ball, surface, court size, boundaries) 'dictate player behaviours and skills they develop' (p. 3)
- 'Nuanced receiving and scanning behaviours' regarded as important football TD skills (p. 1)
- High game speed, most notably during transitions (possession exchanged) (p. 1)
- 'Futsal is characterised by high game speed, most notably during transitions' (p. 1)

**在 Theme 1 中的角色：** 实践验证 — 教练视角确认 futsal 对 football TD 的正向作用。

## Theme 2: EVIDENCE — 迁移实证

### 2.1 Oppici, Panchuk, Serpiello and Farrow (2018) — 传球迁移 RCT

- Futsal group (n=24) improved passing accuracy ES=0.75±0.61 from futsal-like → soccer-like task (p. 949)
- Futsal group more accurate than soccer group in soccer task: ES=2.98±2.96 (p. 949)
- Soccer group passing accuracy stable across tasks: ES=0.10±0.52 (p. 949)
- 'Higher magnitude of transfer from small playing area with short time to larger area with longer time than vice-versa' (p. 949)
- 'Futsal group showed higher adaptation of attention orientation to affordances that emerged with soccer task constraints' (p. 949)
- Attention orientation adaptability = 'one of the main mechanisms promoting skill transfer' (p. 949)
- 'Introduce futsal task constraints to fast-track players' ability to functionally adapt perception-action coupling' (p. 949)
- Skill learning involves permanent change through goal-directed practice with interacting constraints (p. 947)
- Constraints-led perspective: behaviour emerges from self-organisation of organismic, environmental & task constraints (p. 947)
- Transfer = adapting behaviour to new constraints; evaluated on performance achievement, not 'idealised' movement (p. 947)
- Two 5v5+GK modified games: futsal-like (small area + futsal ball) vs soccer-like (large area + soccer ball) (p. 948)

**在 Theme 2 中的角色：** 最直接的因果证据（RCT），futsal 条件 → soccer 条件的传球迁移，ES=2.98。

---

### 2.2 Machado, González-Víllora and Teoldo (2024) — 决策技能实证

- HS group (high decision-making skill): more futsal practice hours in early adolescence (13-15yr) — critical window for DM development (p. 762)
- Deliberate practice in futsal associated ONLY with offensive DM skills (with ball & near ball) — small to medium ES (p. 763)
- Deliberate PLAY in soccer → quicker offensive AND defensive DM speed (medium ES) (p. 763)
- 'Deliberate practice in soccer and deliberate play in soccer are associated with DIFFERENT dimensions of DM skills' (p. 764)
- HS group: more total practice hours in all developmental periods compared to LS group (p. 761)
- DMSP framework: developmental activities = deliberate practice (structured) + deliberate play (unstructured, high-satisfaction) (p. 757)
- First study to examine female professional players' developmental pathways in relation to DM skills (p. 757)
- 'Specialised diversification pathway' best characterises better decision-makers during childhood (6-12yr) (p. 763)
- Deliberate play (informal, high-satisfaction) contributed to DM speed (p. 763)

**在 Theme 2 中的角色：** 从"传球"扩展到"决策"——futsal 经历与进攻决策技能显著相关，尤其在早期青春期（13-15岁）。

### 2.3 Yiannaki, Barron, Collins and Carling (2020) — 比赛表现分析

- 647 passing events per team per game; ~51 dribbling; 78 set play events (p. 149)
- 77.3% ball receptions with SOLE of foot — a futsal-specific signature technique (p. 149)
- 84.1% passes made with dominant foot → 'strong foot dominance' (p. 149)
- Mean HR during 'court time': 164.8±22.3 bpm = 87.7%±4.4% MHR → 'very vigorous' intensity (p. 149-150)
- Mean peak MHR: 98.3±2.5% (p. 149)
- 2.16±0.25 accelerations & 2.78±0.13 decelerations per player per minute (p. 149)
- Compared to SSG: 5v5 soccer produced MHR% ranges of 82-87% — futsal at upper limit (p. 150)
- High action density (2.16 accel + 2.78 decel events/min) implies frequent decision-making under pressure (p. 149)
- Match analysis enables observation of behaviours in real-world settings → 'authenticity enhances representative design' (p. 148)
- Futsal = 5-a-side indoor sport officially recognised by FIFA/UEFA, ~60 million participants across 170 countries (p. 147)

**在 Theme 2 中的角色：** 真实比赛数据佐证——国际赛事中观测的 futsal 表现模式，动作频率和生理负荷接近甚至超过 football SSG 上限。

## Theme 3: HOW — 训练方法 + 注意事项

### 3.1 Oppici, Panchuk, Serpiello and Farrow (2018) — 约束操控方案

- 'Soccer practitioners should introduce futsal task constraints to fast-track perception-action coupling' (p. 949)
- Coach takeaway: manipulate constraints (space, time, opponent pressure) — the constraint design, not the sport label, drives transfer (p. 953)
- Small playing area = reduced time to act → higher perceptual-motor demand (p. 949)
- Recreational participants (13.6yr) → generalisability to elite youth needs caution (p. 953)
- Two 5v5+GK modified games: futsal-like (small area + futsal ball) vs soccer-like (large area + soccer ball) (p. 948)

**具体方法：** 缩小场地面积 + 使用低弹 futsal 球 + 5v5+GK 小场比赛

**在 Theme 3 中的角色：** 给出可操作的约束操控方案。

### 3.2 Nunes, Gonçalves, Davids, Esteves and Travassos (2021) — 年龄段差异

**实验：** 4v4 控球小场比赛，三种场地尺寸（20×15m, 25×20m, 30×25m），三组年龄段

| 年龄段 | 大场地效果 (30×25m) | 小场地效果 (20×15m) | 关键洞察 |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|---------|
| **U11** | 距离覆盖↑（所有速度区）、冲刺↑、RPE↑、技术动作↑ | 感知负荷更高（尽管实际物理负荷低） | 大场地同时促进体能+技术 |
| **U15** | 冲刺↑、最大冲刺速度↑ | — | 场地效应减弱，过渡期 |
| **U23** | RPE↑、冲刺↑ | **传球次数↑**（小场地反而更多传球） | 与 U11 完全相反的模式 |

原文引用：

- U11: large area → MORE distance in all speed zones, sprint number, RPE (all p<.001) (p. 174)
- U23: large area → more RPE & sprints (p<.01). BUT more passing in SMALL area (p. 175)
- U15: 'impact of different playing areas was reduced' — least sensitive to area manipulation (p. 175)
- No significant differences on technical actions across playing areas, but pairwise comparisons showed effects (p. 174)
- U11: MORE passing in LARGE area (30×25m). U23: MORE passing in SMALL area (20×15m) — opposite patterns (p. 175)
- U11 perceived small area as MORE intense despite lower physical load (p. 175)
- 'Large playing areas associated with increase in exercise intensity; small areas foster technical development' (p. 171)
- 'SSCGs need to be designed according to players' individual capabilities' — NOT one-size-fits-all (p. 171)
- Age-specific dimensions: U11→30×25m for max technical+physical; U23→20×15m for max technical density (p. 176)
- 'Pitch size should be scaled to player AGE and individual capabilities' (p. 176)
- First study to show AGE × AREA interaction in SSCGs — methodology replicable for futsal-specific studies (p. 177)

**在 Theme 3 中的角色：** 为 Oppici"缩小场地"的方法提供年龄段实证——证明 U11/U15/U23 对场地尺寸反应完全不同，不能一刀切。

### 3.3 Travassos, Araújo and Davids (2018) — 原则边界

- 'Coaching interventions should highlight informational constraints to improve perception-action coupling' (p. 66)
- Futsal as 10-20% of total training volume in early diversification phase (U10-U14) (p. 69)
- Not all futsal skills transfer positively: sole control, toe pokes may not benefit football (p. 68)
- 'Complementary transfer of capabilities' requires emphasising performance-based affordances (p. 69)
- 'Coach education gap: most coaches don't understand how to design constraints for transfer' (p. 69)
- Futsal = one of multiple formats providing diverse physical & perceptual experiences (p. 68)

**三个核心原则：**
1. **补充非替代：** Futsal 占训练总量 10-20%，不是 100%（U10-U14 阶段）
2. **并非所有技能都迁移：** 脚底控球（sole control）、脚尖捅球（toe pokes）可能无益于 football
3. **教练教育缺口：** 大多数教练不懂如何设计约束条件来促进迁移

**在 Theme 3 中的角色：** 给出具体的剂量建议、迁移边界和教练教育提醒。
